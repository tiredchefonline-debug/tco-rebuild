Privacy Policy

Clear information about the information you share.

This policy explains what this website collects, how it is used, and the third-party services involved.

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This policy describes this website’s current practices. If a project requires different tools or data handling, those details should be addressed in the applicable agreement.

Information you provide

When you submit the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, business name, website address, service interests, and message. When you email directly, the contents of that email and your contact information are received.

Information collected automatically

The hosting provider may process basic technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs. TiredChefOnline does not currently advertise a separate behavioral analytics tracker on this site.

How information is used

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule conversations
  • To understand the requested service and prepare recommendations
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

Service providers

This website uses service providers that may process information on their own systems:

  • Netlify hosts the website and processes form submissions.
  • Calendly provides the optional scheduling widget on the contact page.
  • Google Maps provides the embedded map on the locations page.

When you use an embedded service, that provider’s terms and privacy policy also apply. You can avoid the scheduling widget by using the contact form or email instead.

Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be shared with service providers that support the website or business operations, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and site security.

Retention and security

Inquiry information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for communication, business records, security, or legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to review, correct, or delete information submitted through this website, subject to any information that must be retained for legal or legitimate business reasons.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email tiredchefonline@gmail.com.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the website or its services change. The updated date at the top of the page shows when the latest revision was posted.
